What Does “Asexual” Mean?

Asexuality, often shortened to “Ace,” generally refers to experiencing little or no sexual attraction. Asexuality is a spectrum, and people can experience it in different ways.

Asexual people can still experience romantic relationships, close friendships, and fulfilling lives.

Learn More About Asexuality

What Does “Aromantic” Mean?

Aromanticism, often shortened to “Aro,” generally refers to experiencing little or no romantic attraction. Aromantic people can still form meaningful friendships, family relationships, and other close connections.

Aromanticism is also a spectrum, and experiences can vary from person to person.

Learn More About Aromanticism
IT'S IMPORTANT TO KNOW

Ace ≠ Aro

Asexuality and aromanticism are different experiences. A person can be asexual without being aromantic, aromantic without being asexual, both, or neither.

ATTRACTION CAN TAKE DIFFERENT FORMS

Understanding Attraction

People can experience different types of attraction, and these can exist in different combinations.

Sexual Attraction

Aesthetic Attraction

Finding someone visually appealing without necessarily wanting a romantic or sexual relationship.

IDENTITY & LABELS

You Don't Have to Choose a Label.

Labels can help people understand and describe their experiences, but nobody is required to identify with one. Some people may use an Ace or Aro label, others may use a different label, several labels, or none at all.

What matters most is that each person has the freedom to understand and describe their own experience.
