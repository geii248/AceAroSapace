COMMUNITY

Find spaces where
you can belong.

Community can look like a friend group, an online space, an organization, a classroom, or simply people who understand.

Supportive communities

Look for spaces that respect Ace and Aro identities without pressuring people into romance, sex, or specific labels.

Start conversations

Ask respectful questions, listen to people's experiences, and avoid assuming that one person's identity represents everyone.

Respect boundaries

Consent, privacy, and personal boundaries matter in every kind of relationship and community.

COMMUNITY PRINCIPLE

Connection does not have one required shape.

Friendship, family, partnership, collaboration, and chosen community can all be meaningful.
