WHY DOES VISIBILITY MATTER?

Understanding creates
a more inclusive world.

Ace and Aro experiences are often misunderstood because society commonly assumes that everyone experiences romantic and sexual attraction. This can lead to stereotyping, pressure, and invalidation. Learning about different experiences helps build a more respectful and inclusive society.
